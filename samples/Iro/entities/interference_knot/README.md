# interference_knot

Inharmonic carrier crossings shaped by the two interference fields

Synthesis approach: additive_inharmonic_cross_modulation

Equation: y(t)=E(t)*sum_k a_k*sin(2*pi*f_k*t + beta*sin(2*pi*f_m*t+phi_2)); f_k/f_0={1,1+|c1|,1.5+c2/3,2.01}

Crossing inharmonic partials; variation spans low beating through dense sidebands.

The six variations span different registers, durations, brightness and source-feature rows.
Every WAV is deterministic from its recorded seed and source features.
