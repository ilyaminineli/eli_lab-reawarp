# recurrence_ghost

Feature-indexed microfragments recur at irrationally spaced delays with changing polarity

Synthesis approach: granular_recurrence_with_irrational_delays

Equation: y(t)=sum_k (-1)^k*a_k*g(t-k*delta_k); delta_k=T*(0.13+fract(k*sqrt(2)+coordinate))

Short grains recur at irrationally offset delays with changing polarity and spectral decay.

The six variations span different registers, durations, brightness and source-feature rows.
Every WAV is deterministic from its recorded seed and source features.
