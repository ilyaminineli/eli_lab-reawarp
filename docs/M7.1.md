# M7.1 — Section-Aware Material and Motif Guidance

**Implemented first increment:** the section plan guides kick-anchor presence, thins supporting snare/hat activity in sparse sections, gates texture and ambience by section role, and can omit or register-shift the generated melody.

**Still incomplete:** motif_mode is a section-level label, not a general motif-object graph. A complete motif model must preserve and compare rhythm, contour, duration, articulation, register, timbre, and spatial traits. Retrograde, inversion, fragmentation, recombination, candidate scoring, and audition/freeze remain future work.

Section brightness now scales the FM voice's profile low-pass cutoff. Section instability adds a small bounded drift to FM depth and detune. Both controls preserve the source event contour; neither inserts extra notes.
