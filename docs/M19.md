# M19 — Harmonic context and voice-leading

**Status:** In progress — schema, pitch-field evaluation, sonority model, and deterministic voice-leading foundations are being introduced.  
**Depends on:** M17 canonical Score; M18 control bus remains optional.

## Goal

Give the composition system optional harmonic context, sonority relationships, bass-direction planning and configurable voice-leading while preserving non-functional, modal, intervallic, spectral, microtonal and deliberately ambiguous music.

## Design principles

1. Harmonic context is an optional constraint/weight field, never mandatory chord progression.
2. A pitch collection is vocabulary, not an assumed tonal centre. Centre, stability, emphasis and harmonic rhythm are separate controls.
3. Hard constraints and soft preferences are explicit and inspectable. No single opaque beauty score.
4. Bass is an independent compositional role that may reinforce, interlock with, or oppose the kick.
5. All pitch-to-frequency conversion uses Score tuning reference data. MIDI 1.0 loss is documented rather than hidden.
6. Existing scores with no harmonic context retain their current chromatic behaviour.

## M19.1 — Pitch field and sonority primitives

### Pitch field
- Optional context ID and mode: chromatic, collection, centred, intervallic, spectral, microtonal, ambiguous.
- Optional centre/reference pitch in fractional pitch coordinates.
- Pitch collection and interval vocabulary.
- Per-pitch and per-interval stability/tension weights.
- Register bounds, preferred registers and role-specific ranges.
- Harmonic rhythm/sonority change positions in rational beats.
- Explicit absence of a centre or context is valid.

### Sonority
- Stable ID and onset/duration in rational beats.
- Pitch members in tuning-aware pitch coordinates, not pre-rounded MIDI notes.
- Optional bass/root reference, register/spread, tension metadata, and source/transformation provenance.
- Relations: retain, rotate, transpose, invert intervals, expand/contract, registral displacement, spectral derive, or replace.

### Score persistence
- Extend Score with an optional serializable harmonic-context/sonority section using a backward-compatible default.
- Validate unique IDs, rational timing, finite weights, legal bounds, and tuning references.
- Preserve absent context exactly through load/save round-trip.

## M19.2 — Tuning-aware pitch field

- Resolve frequency from Score tuning_reference_pitch, tuning_reference_frequency_hz, tuning_divisions_per_period, and a pitch-field coordinate.
- Support equal divisions of an octave as a baseline and permit non-octave period ratios through explicit tuning definitions.
- Expose candidate pitch weights and reject candidates only when a hard constraint says so.
- Keep soft tension/stability as explanatory values, not hard-coded tonal correction.
- Preserve the ordinary 12-TET path and current chromatic default.

## M19.3 — Sonority and voice-leading planner

- Plan a sequence of sonorities from section/phrase boundaries.
- Assign melodic voices across adjacent sonorities using configurable costs: motion distance, large leaps, register crossing, voice crossing, common-tone retention and target-role preference.
- Support contrary, oblique, parallel, common-tone and deliberate crossing preferences.
- Make cost weights and hard constraints user-visible and serializable.
- Deterministic stable tie-breaking must not depend on hash iteration order.
- Permit intentionally high friction and unresolved motion.

## M19.4 — Bass role planner

Independent bass modes:
- pedal/drone;
- kick-locked reinforcement;
- syncopated ostinato;
- independent cycle;
- melodic bass;
- punctuation/answer gestures.

Bass plan uses shared phrase/form boundaries but owns its rhythm and pitch movement. Density, register, movement range, kick relationship and cadence avoidance are explicit controls.

## M19.5 — Motif and MIDI integration

- Motif transformations declare which identity dimensions are retained: contour, interval profile, rhythm, register, articulation and timbre.
- Sonority changes can transform a motif without erasing its identity.
- Resolve all pitch values through the Score tuning reference.
- MIDI 1.0 exports rounded integer semitone note numbers with exact Reawarp pitch/frequency metadata retained in text events. External MIDI players cannot reproduce arbitrary microtonal tuning without pitch-bend/MPE conventions.

## Acceptance criteria

- Existing chromatic projects render unchanged without harmonic context.
- Context and sonority records serialize/round-trip deterministically.
- Frequency calculations are mathematically correct for equal divisions and explicit period ratios.
- Pitch-field hard constraints are never violated; soft costs remain separately inspectable.
- Voice-leading assignments are deterministic, honor crossing/leap constraints, and expose their cost breakdown.
- Bass patterns can move independently of percussion while retaining common phrase/form timing.
- Tests cover centred, modal, intervallic, spectral, microtonal and ambiguous fields plus MIDI precision boundaries.

## Listening protocol

Use one motif/rhythm with: unconstrained chromatic pitch, pedal-centred pitch, slowly changing sonorities, intervallic field, spectral field and non-12TET tuning. Level-match and assess continuity, tension, melodic identity, bass/percussion relationship, and whether harmonic direction emerges without over-explaining the material.

## Out of scope

Mandatory functional chord progressions, automatic tonal correction, opaque AI scoring, a DAW piano roll, and compulsory harmony on every score.

## Implementation sequence

- **M19.1:** schema + validation + serialization + pitch-field frequency math.
- **M19.2:** candidate generation and sonority transformations.
- **M19.3:** configurable voice-leading assignment and bass role grammar.
- **M19.4:** motif integration, MIDI precision/reporting, UI exposure and listening fixtures.
