# M19 — Harmonic context and voice-leading

**Status:** Planned  
**Depends on:** M17 canonical Score; M18 optional automation targets.

## Goal

Develop a pitch and sonority system that can provide coherent harmonic context, bass motion and voice-leading without forcing every Reawarp composition into functional tonality.

## Philosophy

The guides distinguish pitch vocabulary from pitch hierarchy. A restricted pitch collection does not automatically create a tonal centre; hierarchy can emerge through register, duration, repetition, bass emphasis, phrase placement and timbre. Reawarp should support centred, modal, intervallic, spectral, microtonal and deliberately ambiguous pitch languages.

## Scope

1. **Harmonic context model**
   - Optional serializable pitch field, centre/reference, pitch collection, allowed intervals, stability/tension weights and harmonic rhythm.
   - Sonority objects describe pitch set, bass/root role, register, duration, tension and transformation.
   - Context can be static, section-changing, slowly evolving or intentionally absent.

2. **Pitch hierarchy and constraints**
   - Weight pitches/intervals by role, register, duration, repetition and formal position.
   - Constrain candidate generation without forcing all notes to resolve.
   - Allow controlled dissonance, chromatic friction, spectral relations and ambiguous centres.

3. **Voice-leading**
   - Assign notes between adjacent sonorities with configurable motion, crossing and leap costs.
   - Support common tones, contrary/oblique motion, registral displacement and deliberate voice crossing.
   - Keep the cost function visible and user-configurable; do not present it as a universal beauty score.

4. **Bass as an independent compositional role**
   - Pedal, kick-locked, syncopated ostinato, independent cycle, melodic bass and punctuation.
   - Allow bass and kick to reinforce, interlock or deliberately disagree.

5. **Motif and tuning integration**
   - Preserve or transform selected motif identity dimensions: contour, interval profile, rhythm, register, articulation and timbre.
   - Resolve frequency from the Score tuning reference and divisions rather than assuming semitone equal temperament.
   - Define how non-12TET data maps to MIDI 1.0 and document losses.

## Explicitly out of scope

A mandatory chord progression engine, automatic tonal correction, opaque AI scoring and a DAW piano roll.

## Acceptance criteria

- Default chromatic behaviour stays available and backwards compatible.
- Harmonic contexts serialize and round-trip.
- Frequencies are deterministic and mathematically correct for each tuning map.
- Voice-leading assignments respect declared hard constraints and produce stable tie-breaking.
- Bass role patterns can be independent from percussion while retaining a common phrase/form plan.
- Tests cover tonal-centred, modal, intervallic, spectral, microtonal and ambiguous contexts, including MIDI precision boundaries.

## Listening protocol

Use the same motif and rhythmic arrangement with: unconstrained chromatic pitch, pedal-centred pitch, slowly changing sonorities, intervallic context and a non-12TET field. Level-match and assess continuity, tension, melodic identity and whether the harmonic layer adds direction without over-explaining the material.

## Deliverables

- Harmonic/sonority context schema.
- Optional pitch hierarchy and voice-leading planner.
- Bass role planner.
- Tuning-aware pitch resolution tests and guide.
