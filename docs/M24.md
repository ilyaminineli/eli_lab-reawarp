# M24 — Advanced DSP research modules

**Status:** Planned research track  
**Depends on:** M21 sound-object contracts and M22 mixer/tail/latency semantics.

## Goal

Introduce advanced DSP as independently scoped optional tools after sound-object and mixer contracts are stable.

## Independent modules

Each module must be planned, implemented and accepted separately:

1. **Convolution and designed impulse-response instruments** — convolution reverb, spectral imprinting and designed resonant responses.
2. **Spectral delay** — frequency-dependent delay, spectral feedback and controlled bin/region movement.
3. **Cross-synthesis and frequency transformation** — ring/frequency modulation, vocoder/cross-synthesis and inharmonic translation.
4. **Oversampled nonlinear processing** — saturation, clipping and waveshaping with explicit anti-aliasing/oversampling policy.
5. **Sidechain and envelope routing** — detector filtering, envelope extraction, smoothing, hysteresis and cross-stem control.
6. **Feedback networks** — FDNs and related recursive structures with stability/headroom bounds.
7. **Optional parallel processing branches** — only where a measured musical use-case justifies the routing complexity.

## Required module specification

- Musical purpose and representative arrangement use-case.
- Algorithm/reference implementation and parameter units/ranges.
- Sample-rate, oversampling and block-processing policy.
- Latency, alignment, tail and feedback-stability policy.
- CPU/memory budget and representative benchmarks.
- Deterministic random-stream policy.
- Headroom, denormal, finite-output and mono compatibility handling.
- Optional dependency/native runtime and licensing review.
- Portable schema, bypass behaviour and regression fixtures.

## Acceptance criteria

- Each module renders offline without mutating the input.
- Latency/tail/CPU/memory are measured and recorded.
- Deterministic or documented best-effort reproducibility holds for its contract.
- CI covers reference fixtures and signal safety without mandatory native runtimes.
- A level-matched listening example shows a clear compositional use; otherwise the module remains experimental.

## Not in scope

One all-encompassing DSP release, mandatory third-party dependencies, GPU processing without profiling, or arbitrary feedback graphs.
